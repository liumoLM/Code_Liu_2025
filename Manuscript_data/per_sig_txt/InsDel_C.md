In the 89-type classification, InsDel_C looks somewhat similar to InsDel13 in the prominence of deletions of Ts that are followed by A.  However, the 83-type and 476-type classifications show that InsDel13 has deletions of from TT to T (e.g. ATTA -> ATA), while InsDel_C has deletions of single isolated  Ts (e.g. ATA -> AA).

Nevertheless, InsDel13 and InsDel_C were extracted separately in the 89-type classification, possibly because of the insertion of isolated Ts in InsDel13 but not InsDel_C
