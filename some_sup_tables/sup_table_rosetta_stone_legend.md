Indel categories in the 476-type-classification and corresponding categories in the 89 and 83-type classification schemes, along with examples of indels in each classification (column "Indel in context"). With two exceptions, each 476-type indel category maps to a single 89-type category. The exceptions are Del(6,):U2:R(4,9) and Del(6,):U2:R(4,9), each of which maps to both Del(2,):U(1,2):R(5,) and Del(2,8):U(1,2):R(2,4). The column "Indel in context" shows the inserted or deleted sequence surround by "<" and ">" and in bold pink font.  We standardized the representation by moving the insertion of deletion as far to the left as possible (see function justify_indel() in package mSigSpectra). Additional repeats of the entire inserted or deleted sequence are indicated in pink, with alternating repeat unit underlined. Microhomologies are indicated by paired double underscores. One of the regions is always at inside the insertion or deletion, at the far left, and and the other region, identical to the first, appears in black immediately to the right of the indel.
